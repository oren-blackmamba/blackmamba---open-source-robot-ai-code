**AURA** (Autonomous Ultra-Responsive Android) is an advanced, fully open-source humanoid robot designed to bridge the gap between High-Level AI Reasoning (Embodied Intelligence) and Ultra-Precise Physical Coordination.

Designed for researchers, engineers, and robotics enthusiasts, AURA leverages state-of-the-art vision-language-action (VLA) models, real-time reinforcement learning, and high-frequency dynamic balance control to navigate and interact with complex human environments naturally.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Capabilities

* **🧠 Embodied AI & VLA Integration:**
  Powered by Vision-Language-Action models and LLM reasoning for high-level decision making, spatial understanding, and intuitive natural voice interaction.

* **🤸 High-Precision Coordination & Whole-Body Control:**
  Advanced Inverse Kinematics (IK), Dynamic Balance, and Whole-Body Control (WBC) operating at 1kHz for smooth, agile, and robust bipedal walking and complex manipulation tasks.

* **👁️ Real-Time Spatial Perception:**
  3D Environment mapping, obstacle avoidance, and precise object tracking using stereo depth cameras, IMU sensor fusion, and real-time computer vision.

* **🎮 Simulation-First Workflow:**
  Full support for physics simulations in **NVIDIA Isaac Sim** and **ROS 2 / Gazebo**, enabling risk-free training, testing, and evaluation before deploying to physical hardware.

* **⚙️ Modular & Scalable Architecture:**
  Decoupled layers for Hardware Control, Kinematics, Perception, and High-Level Reasoning to allow seamless hardware upgrades and component-level research.
